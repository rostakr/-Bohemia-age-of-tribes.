# Art Bible

## Identity

**BOHEMIA: AGE OF TRIBES** is a professional, historically grounded 3D RTS. Its landscapes and settlements should feel inhabited, practical, and specific to Bohemia. The tone is human and material: timber, earth, iron, cloth, smoke, water, worked fields, and regional trade.

**Tagline:** Forge a tribe. Rule the land. Shape Bohemia.

## Historical pillars

| Culture and period | Visual and social emphasis |
| --- | --- |
| Boii, Late La Tène | Trade, skilled metallurgy, oppida, defensible earthworks, and connected workshops |
| Marcomanni, early Roman Imperial | Prestige goods, raids, warrior retinues, and selective Roman contact |
| Slavs, 6th–7th centuries | Settlement networks, agriculture, household production, and small-scale timber building |

The campaign presents these periods chronologically. A skirmish mixing them must state plainly that it is anachronistic. Do not use fantasy motifs or generic medieval castles as visual shorthand.

## Landscape benchmark

Phase 1 targets south/central Bohemia: rolling terrain, a readable stream, mixed deciduous forest, meadow, and a worn path. The scene must support strategic readability while retaining natural variation. Its population benchmark is three Boii structures and five inhabitants.

## Materials, scale, and readability

- Favor plausible local materials, restrained color, weathering from use, and construction that exposes how objects are made.
- Keep building silhouettes distinct by function and construction rather than exaggerated ornament.
- Keep people, doors, tools, vegetation, and terrain at a consistent human scale.
- Reserve strong contrast and saturation for selection, ownership, danger, and other gameplay signals.
- Resources must read as food, wood, stone, iron, and trade wealth without fantasy effects.

## Architecture and characters

Use timber, wattle and daub, thatch, earthworks and period-appropriate stone. Boii workshops and fortified centres, Marcomanni halls and retinue settings, and early Slav dispersed households need distinct reference briefs; no invented certainty about archaeological reconstruction. Gates and palisades must communicate access and defense at the intended camera height.

Characters use a controlled modular set of bodies, garments, hair, equipment and accessories. Role readability comes from silhouette, carried tool/weapon, shield and animation. Do not randomize equipment across periods; do not rely on tiny surface details for gameplay identification. Animation must eventually show the actual task and carried load.

## Environment and material rules

Prioritize oak, beech, birch and locally plausible occasional conifers, meadows, muddy paths, wetlands and rocky outcrops. Keep paths and work areas readable beneath canopy. Use LOD, instancing and reduced distant detail when actual assets arrive.

Production materials use base color, normals and roughness, plus metallic/AO where relevant: rough thatch, fibrous wood, earthen walls, restrained iron highlights and wet mud variation. Calibration solid colors are not an exception for production content. Natural sun, environment light and restrained fog/postprocessing must preserve unit contrast; gameplay must remain readable in overcast light.

## Prohibited motifs

No Gothic towers, generic fantasy taverns, medieval stone castles, fantasy vegetation, oversized ornamental armour or copied proprietary assets/UI. Avoid Mediterranean or generic American landscape cues, excessive bloom, chromatic aberration, gameplay depth-of-field and cinematic darkness that obscures orders or units.

## Phase 0 boundary

Phase 0 contains only a clearly labeled disposable primitive calibration floor and height marker in a static inspection view. They establish rendering, scale, and lifecycle behavior; they are not an art direction target or production assets. No professional art assets are imported in this phase.

Commercial assets require review of provenance, license, historical fit, visual fit, technical fit, and modification needs in `ASSET_MANIFEST.md` before purchase, import, or mass production.
